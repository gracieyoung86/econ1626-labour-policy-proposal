# ECON1626 Labour Policy Proposal

## Assessment 2: Policy Proposal

This repository contains my ECON1626 Economics of Artificial Intelligence Assessment 2 submission.

The project examines how the Australian Government should respond to AI-driven changes in the labour market, with a focus on worker adjustment, reskilling and evidence-based policy targeting.

## Policy Client

Australian Government Department of Employment and Workplace Relations (DEWR).

## Repository Structure

- `proposal.md` – Main policy proposal
- `reflection.md` – AI use reflection
- `README.md` – Repository overview and navigation

## Policy Focus

The proposal recommends combining:

1. AI Skills and Transition Accounts for workers in occupations experiencing significant AI-related task change.
2. A National AI Labour-Market Early Warning System led by DEWR and Jobs and Skills Australia.

The package is designed to target support where labour-market adjustment pressures are strongest while preserving the productivity benefits of AI adoption.

# AI Use Reflection

I utilised ChatGPT throughout this assessment mainly for technical guidance, drafting support, editing and help with Git/GitHub. It helped me set up Git, create the repository structure, understand Markdown formatting and organise the policy proposal into the required sections. I also used it to suggest draft wording and help tighten sections to meet the word limit. Key claims were checked against government and academic sources not relied on AI-generated summaries as my main evidence source.

One specific example of editing AI output was the policy costing. The initial draft presented broad cost ranges, but I changed the wording, so rather than official government estimates the figures were clearly identified as analytical assumptions. I also revised the worker-training costing so the assumptions about participant numbers and administration were clear.

A limitation was that AI can produce plausible-sounding figures, citations or policy claims that still needed to be verified. I had to check sources, revise unsupported information and avoid presenting assumptions as true. Overall, AI was most useful for editing, structure and technical support.
